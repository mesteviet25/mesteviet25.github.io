Collision Lab

Set the pitch, the swing, and where the bat meets the ball. The model collides them and flies the result: exit velo, launch angle, spray, the spin the ball leaves with, and where it lands. The smash factor sets how much energy a dead-center hit returns; the table compares the same swing at other smash factors.

Inches the ball sits above (+) or below (-) the barrel's center line. + = bat under the ball.

Model: collision efficiency q from the smash factor (EV = q · pitch + (1 + q) · bat speed, after Alan Nathan's formulation), a gripping-ball friction impulse for spin (ball I = 0.4 mR², friction 0.5), and a drag + Magnus flight at sea level with no wind. The bat's long axis sits in the swing plane, so tilt sets how far the barrel drops below the hands. A teaching model, not a fit to tracked data.
